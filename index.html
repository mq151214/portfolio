06 / About

I don't just find vulnerabilities. I design the systems that close them.

A bit about how I got here, and what I'm working on now.

Most people learn security by studying theory. I learned it by breaking things — then understanding why they broke.

I'm a Computer Science graduate from HITEC University, Taxila — currently based in Islamabad, Pakistan — working at the intersection of AI and cybersecurity. My work focuses on building systems that don't just detect threats — they understand them.

During my Red Team internship at ITSOLERA PVT LTD, I worked on penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, and offensive security tooling. That experience taught me something most developers never learn: how attackers actually think. Now I use that mindset to build defenses.

I'm certified in Network Security by Cisco Network Academy and in Ethical Hacking by ITSOLERA — both verifiable credentials that back up what I build.

I believe ethical hacking isn't a contradiction — it's the highest form of engineering. You have to understand a system deeply enough to break it before you can make it genuinely secure.
